Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name, email address, and password. As you use the Service, we also store the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide and improve the Service — for example, to display your expense history, generate category breakdowns and monthly summaries, and keep your account secure. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, but no method of storage or transmission over the internet is guaranteed to be 100% secure.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may use limited third-party services (such as hosting or infrastructure providers) solely to operate the Service, and any such providers are only given the access necessary to perform their function.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on the Spendly website.
